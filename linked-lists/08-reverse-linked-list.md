# Reverse Linked List

**Problem:** Reverse a singly linked list.

**Approach:** Use prev, curr, and 
ext pointers to reverse each link iteratively.

**Test Cases:**
- 1 ? 2 ? 3 ? 4 ? 4 ? 3 ? 2 ? 1
- 1 ? 1

**Time Complexity:** O(n)
**Space Complexity:** O(1)
